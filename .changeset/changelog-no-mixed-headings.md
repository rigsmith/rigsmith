A changelog entry with typed changes no longer mixes heading styles: its untyped changes join the typed section their bump stands for (a major the 💥 Breaking section, a minor the `feat` group's, a patch, "Updated dependencies" included, the `fix` group's) instead of adding `Minor Changes` and `Patch Changes` headings beside 🚀 Enhancements. An entry with no typed changes renders as @changesets does, unchanged, and a `none` change no longer renders a `### None Changes` heading in either style.
